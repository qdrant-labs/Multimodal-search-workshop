# Evidence Standards

Use this skill as a retrieval and reporting assistant, not as a causal inference engine.

- Treat a chunk as **direct** evidence only when the quote itself contains the core topic terms or close synonyms.
- Treat a chunk as **contextual** evidence when semantic search finds a relevant passage but the exact claim is implicit.
- Treat a chunk as a **lead** when it is useful for follow-up but too indirect to support a published claim.
- Always preserve ticker, company, speaker, date, point ID, and audio clip path when available.
- Prefer a balanced brief: evidence for the claim, possible counter-evidence, and explicit gaps.
- Do not publish a transcript quote until the audio clip or full source transcript has been checked.
